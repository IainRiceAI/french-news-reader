Neuf hommes et trois femmes, âgés de 19 à 61 ans, ont été arrêtés à Teesside, soupçonnés de diverses infractions, notamment la possession d'une arme à feu et l'appartenance à un groupe du crime organisé.

Nine men and three women, aged between 19 and 61, have been arrested across Teesside on suspicion of a range of offences including possession of a firearm and being part of an organised crime group.

Aux premières heures de samedi, une Volkswagen Passat poursuivie par la police a emprunté à contresens l'A66 près de Middlesbrough et a percuté une voiture de police venant en sens inverse - deux PC et les cinq occupants de la Passat sont morts.

In the early hours of Saturday, a Volkswagen Passat that had been pursued by police travelled the wrong way down the A66 near Middlesbrough and crashed into an oncoming police car - two PCs and all five Passat occupants died.

La police de Cleveland a déclaré qu'elle enquêtait sur "une activité criminelle grave ayant abouti à cet incident mortel".

Cleveland Police said it was investigating "serious criminal activity that resulted in this fatal incident".

Le Bureau indépendant pour la conduite de la police (IOPC) a ouvert une enquête sur la collision suite à une auto-saisine de la police de Cleveland, qui est obligatoire lorsqu'une personne décède suite à un contact avec la police.

The Independent Office for Police Conduct (IOPC) has launched an investigation into the collision following a self-referral from Cleveland Police, which is mandatory when someone dies following contact with police.

Le FIPOL a déclaré que les agents avaient suivi la Passat pendant près de sept minutes avant d'abandonner la poursuite lorsque la voiture a pris la mauvaise direction sur l'A66 à South Bank.

The IOPC said officers had followed the Passat for almost seven minutes before abandoning the pursuit when the car went the wrong way down the A66 at South Bank.

Dix secondes plus tard, à 03h39 BST, la Passat est entrée en collision avec le véhicule des PJ Clough et Blades, qui circulaient du bon côté de la route et se trouvaient dans la zone pour aider à la recherche de la voiture.

Ten seconds later at 03:39 BST, the Passat collided with the vehicle of PCs Clough and Blades, who were travelling on the correct side of the road and were in the area to assist with the search for the car.

The government called for social media platforms to remove posts celebrating such behaviour.

La police de Cleveland a déclaré : "À l'heure actuelle, il n'y a aucune preuve indiquant que le tournage des activités qui ont eu lieu vendredi soir et samedi matin a eu lieu aux fins de TikTok."

Cleveland Police said: "At the current time, there is no evidence to indicate that filming of the activities that took place on Friday night and Saturday morning has taken place for the purposes of TikTok."

PC Blades' wife said he "gave his life in the line of duty, protecting and serving others with courage, dedication and selflessness".

The college said: "Many within our community will remember Matty as an intelligent, kind and approachable teacher, a valued colleague and someone who gave generously of himself to the young people in his care."

They said he "jumped into action whenever needed" and was "willing to help anyone for any reason".

Le communiqué de la famille ajoute qu'il "s'est battu fièrement pour que son pays rende le monde meilleur".

The family statement added that he "proudly fought for his country to make the world a better place".

Flags at Cleveland Police headquarters are flying at half-mast and Redcar and Cleveland Council has opened a book of condolence at its offices on Ridley Street in Redcar.
