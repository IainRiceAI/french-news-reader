# Trump waives several Russia sanctions as row with Zelensky intensifies

**BBC Original Link:** [https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cxr5yvzv7rp0o?at_medium=RSS&at_campaign=rss](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cxr5yvzv7rp0o?at_medium=RSS&at_campaign=rss)

> **BBC Summary:** The US president temporarily ends many restrictions on Moscow recently passed by Congress over "national security" concerns.
