# Four dead and thousands without power after Hurricane Isaias lashes US

**BBC Original Link:** [https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cmje5x80yg70o?at_medium=RSS&at_campaign=rss](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cmje5x80yg70o?at_medium=RSS&at_campaign=rss)  
**Published:** Sat, 10 Oct 2026 23:19:55 GMT  

---

> **BBC Summary:** Roofs were ripped off buildings after Isaias barrelled into the US on Friday evening, before it weakened to a post-tropical cyclone.

---

### Section 1

**🇬🇧 EN:** Four people have died and hundreds of thousands are without power after Hurricane Isaias battered parts of the southern US with powerful winds and heavy rain.

**🇫🇷 FR:** *Quatre personnes sont mortes et des centaines de milliers sont sans électricité après que l'ouragan Isaias ait frappé des parties du sud des États-Unis avec de puissants vents et de fortes pluies.*
