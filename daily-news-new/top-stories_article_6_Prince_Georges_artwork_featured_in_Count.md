# Prince George's artwork featured in Country Life magazine

**BBC Original Link:** [https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c6q8n8n835vqo?at_medium=RSS&at_campaign=rss](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c6q8n8n835vqo?at_medium=RSS&at_campaign=rss)

> **BBC Summary:** The Prince of Wales features his 13-year-old son's drawing of a bird as part of a special edition of the magazine.

---
