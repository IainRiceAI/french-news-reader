# What we know so far after Krakow gun attack

**BBC Original Link:** [https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cmd7q15drwgeo?at_medium=RSS&at_campaign=rss](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cmd7q15drwgeo?at_medium=RSS&at_campaign=rss)  
**Published:** Sun, 08 Nov 2026 01:47:41 GMT

> **BBC Summary:** Six people were killed in a random shooting in the city's main square, and three bodies were later found at the home of the attacker.

### Section 1

**🇬🇧 EN:** At least six people were killed and more than a dozen others injured when a gunman suspected to have ties to the far-right opened fire in central Krakow, the second largest city in Poland.
