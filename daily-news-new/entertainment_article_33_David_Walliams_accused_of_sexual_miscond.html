ITV News et le Guardian ont publié des rapports alléguant que David Walliams a été accusé de délit sexuel par plusieurs femmes, dont une qui avait 15 ans.

ITV News and the Guardian have published reports alleging that David Walliams has been accused of sexual misconduct by several women, including one who was aged 15.

Les allégations ont été faites dans le cadre d'une enquête conjointe de trois ans, externe des deux organisations de presse, qui a été publiée après que l'artiste a perdu une tentative légale pour empêcher les allégations contre lui d'être rendues publiques.

The allegations were made as a part of a joint three-year investigation, external by the two news organisations, which was published after the entertainer lost a legal bid to stop the claims against him being made public.

Certaines femmes accusent Walliams de les avoir "traités avec soin" quand elles étaient adolescentes.

Some of the women accuse Walliams of "grooming" them when they were teenagers. One now characterises her alleged encounter with Walliams at 15 years old as a "sexual assault".

Dans une lettre adressée aux journalistes publiée par le Guardian, Walliams a déclaré qu'il n'était " pas un prédateur sexuel " et qu'il n'avait pas " enfreint la loi ".

In a letter to journalists published by the Guardian, Walliams said he was "not a sexual predator" and had not "broken the law".

Il a dit qu'il " réalisait que parfois je me comportais de façon imprudente envers d'autres personnes avec qui j'avais des relations sexuelles occasionnelles " et qu'il regrette de ne pas toujours avoir " pris correctement en compte les sentiments des gens ".

He said he "realised that at times I behaved thoughtlessly towards other people I had casual sexual relationships with" and said he regretted not always taking "people's feelings properly into account".

Walliams est devenu célèbre aux côtés de Matt Lucas au début des années 2000 avec la comédie de sketch de la BBC Little Britain, et a également été juge de Britain's Got Talent de l'ITV et a lancé une série de livres pour enfants best-sellers.

Walliams shot to fame alongside Matt Lucas in the early 2000s with BBC sketch comedy Little Britain, and also served as a judge on ITV's Britain's Got Talent and launched a best-selling series of children's books.

Le Guardian et ITV News disent que plusieurs des femmes, qui ne se connaissent pas, affirment avoir été victimes d'avancées sexuelles indésirables de la part de Walliams.

The Guardian and ITV News say that several of the women, who do not know one another, allege they were targets of unwanted sexual advances from Walliams.

Dans une lettre adressée aux journalistes travaillant sur l'enquête de l'ITV et du Guardian, Walliams a écrit: "En regardant en arrière, je me rends compte que parfois je me comporte de façon imprudente envers d'autres personnes avec lesquelles j'ai eu des relations sexuelles occasionnelles.

In a letter addressed to journalists working on the ITV and Guardian investigation, Walliams wrote: "Looking back, I realise that at times I behaved thoughtlessly towards other people I had casual sexual relationships with.

"Je me rends compte que je n'ai pas toujours pris correctement en compte les sentiments des gens. C'est quelque chose que je regrette. Et bien qu'un déséquilibre de pouvoir soit inévitable quand une partie est célèbre, l'abus de ce pouvoir est complètement autre chose et c'est quelque chose que je n'ai jamais fait".

"I realise I did not always take people's feelings properly into account. That is something I regret. And whilst a power imbalance is inevitable when one party is famous, abusing that power is something else entirely and it is something that I never did."

Il a ajouté: " Je ne suis pas un prédateur sexuel. Je n'ai pas violé la loi, mais je suis dévasté que certaines des femmes que j'ai rencontrées il y a 10 à 20 ans aient maintenant exprimé leur colère ou leur douleur envers moi. "

He added: "I am not a sexual predator. I have not broken the law, but I am devastated that some of the women I met 10 to 20 years ago have now expressed hurt or anger towards me."

Après l'enquête a été publiée mardi, l'agent littéraire de longue date de Walliams Independent Talent Group a confirmé qu'il l'avait renvoyé.

After the investigation was published on Tuesday, Walliams's long-time literary agent Independent Talent Group confirmed it had dropped him.

Un porte-parole de la BBC a décrit les allégations comme "sérieuses" et la société a retiré Little Britain de l'iPlayer de la BBC.

A BBC spokesperson described the allegations as "serious" and the corporation has removed Little Britain from BBC iPlayer.

Sky a également décrit les allégations comme "extrêmement graves", disant qu'elle abandonnait son adaptation à venir du long métrage de fantaisie de Walliams Fing! et avait "déjà supprimé du contenu qui le présente sur nos plateformes".

Sky also described the allegations as "extremely serious", saying it was dropping its upcoming adaptation of Walliams's fantasy feature Fing! and had "already removed content which features him from our platforms".

La police métropolitaine a déclaré qu'elle avait contacté le Guardian et ITV News pour " s'assurer que les victimes potentielles " soient " informées de la façon dont elles pourraient signaler des allégations criminelles à la police ".

The Metropolitan Police said it has contacted the Guardian and ITV News to "to ensure that any potential victims" are "made aware of how they could report any criminal allegations to police".

She said that after she arrived to stay at his house she "felt instantly pressured to share a bed with him".

J'avais une force intérieure comme: "Non, je ne suis pas attirée par toi de cette façon"... J'ai senti que j'étais manipulée", a- t- elle ajouté.

I had an inner strength like, 'no I'm not attracted to you in that way'... I felt I was being manipulated," she added.

Ils abusent de leur position, utilisent les jeunes et abusent des jeunes, et des gens de tout âge bien sûr. Il y avait encore trop d'hommes au pouvoir, qui font cela et ils doivent partir".

She told Newsnight: "I am angry, I'm suppressing it well, but just that this culture of older men in positions of power needs to go, needs to stop. They're abusing their positions, using young people and abusing young people, and people of any age of course. There were too many men in power still, that are doing this and they need to go."

Les avocats de Walliams ont déclaré à l'enquête ITV News et Guardian qu'il niait avoir préparé Creightmore ou avoir agi inappropriément.

Walliams's lawyers told the ITV News and Guardian investigation that he denied grooming Creightmore or acting inappropriately. They added he did not "target" her because she was "vulnerable" or "young".

Il a déclaré qu'il croyait que son amitié avec Lilly était " spéciale " et se sentait " dévastée " par son compte.

Walliams said he believed his friendship with Lilly was a "special one" and felt "devastated" by her account. He denied the offers of help or gifts were attempts to groom, manipulate or coerce her into sleeping with him.

Two women, given pseudonyms of Alex and Jade in the investigation, have alleged that they first had contact with Walliams when they were 15 years old.

Alex alleged that she met Walliams in 2004 at a fan event when she was 15. She claimed that she and Walliams stayed in touch over the next two years and they had consensual sex for the first time when she was 17, the report alleged.

Walliams denied grooming Alex. He said he is "mortified to learn" that their "brief, consensual relationship has caused her sadness".

Syco Entertainment, the company that created Britain's Got Talent, said it had had "no knowledge of the allegations described and was not made aware of them at the time".

The show's production company, Fremantle UK, said "no complaint has ever been made" against Walliams and encouraged anyone who "feels they have been the subject of any impropriety or wrongdoing by anyone... to come forward".

In a statement responding to ITV News and the Guardian's investigation, the company said it takes employee wellbeing "extremely seriously".

Walliams denies that he sexually harassed anyone at HarperCollins or behaved inappropriately in the workplace.

The two news organisations had argued that they believed it was in the public interest to publish the allegations.

Last week, a High Court judge rejected the injunction request and Walliams did not lodge an appeal, clearing the way for him to be named.
