Met Police investigating more 'potential criminal allegations' about Brand

Summary: Det Supt Andy Furphy says the force had received "several reports during the trial and since the trial".

La police métropolitaine a déclaré que plusieurs personnes les ont contactées pendant le procès et après la condamnation et la condamnation du comédien Russell Brand, "pour fournir des informations ou signaler une allégation criminelle potentielle".

The Metropolitan Police have said several people contacted them during the trial and following the conviction and sentencing of the disgraced comedian Russell Brand, "to provide information or report a potential criminal allegation".

Un porte-parole du Met a déclaré: "Les détectives évaluent ces informations dans le cadre de leurs enquêtes en cours".

A Met spokesperson said: "Detectives are assessing this information as part of their ongoing enquiries."

S'adressant à BBC Breakfast, le détective Andy Furphy, qui a dirigé l'enquête sur Brand, a déclaré que les agents " regardaient exactement ce que nous avions " et " s'il s'agissait d'informations qui indiquaient un comportement ou s'il pouvait constituer une infraction pénale ".

Speaking to BBC Breakfast, Det Supt Andy Furphy, who led the Brand investigation, said officers were "looking at exactly what we've got" and "whether they're information that points to behaviour or whether they could constitute a criminal offence".

Brand est déjà enquêté sur les allégations de viol de deux autres femmes.

Brand is already being investigated over rape allegations from two more women. He has been interviewed under caution in connection to those allegations.

On Monday, Brand was sentenced to 10 years and six months in prison, after being convicted of rape and sexual assault.

Dans ses déclarations de condamnation, le juge Joel Bennathan l'a qualifié de " prédateur sexuel " et a critiqué son " arrogance stupéfiante ".

In his sentencing remarks, judge Joel Bennathan described him as a "sexual predator" and criticised his "staggering arrogance".

The 51-year-old was sentenced to nine years for rape, with an additional 18 months, nine months and eight months for three counts of sexual assault. The latter sentences are to run concurrently.

Il a été reconnu coupable des accusations portées contre quatre femmes entre 2004 et 2009.

He was found guilty of the charges involving four women between 2004 and 2009.

Le chef de la police métropolitaine, Sir Mark Rowley, a déclaré vendredi qu'il "ne serait pas surpris" si davantage de personnes venaient faire des allégations à son sujet.

The head of the Metropolitan Police, Sir Mark Rowley, said on Friday that he "wouldn't be surprised" if more people came forward to make allegations about him.

Interrogée par BBC Breakfast sur la façon dont les femmes qui ont témoigné dans le procès ont réagi, Furphy a déclaré: "Certains ont trouvé cela très décourageant en raison de l'attention des médias qui en dégageait.

Asked by BBC Breakfast how the women in the case who gave evidence in the trial were coping, Furphy said: "Some have found it deeply distressing because of the amount of media attention wrapped around it.

Même Russell Brand, avant et pendant l'épreuve, mettait des documents en ligne, mais nous continuons à les soutenir. "

"Even Russell Brand himself before and during trial was putting material online, but we continue to support them."

Il a noté que cette condamnation "n'aurait pas pu être réalisée" sans leur "courage, leur courage, leur résilience tout au long du processus de justice pénale".

He noted how the conviction "could not have been achieved" without their "bravery, their courage, their resilience throughout the whole criminal justice process".

Il a poursuivi: "C'est profondément personnel et assez douloureux d'être victime de viol et d'agression sexuelle, et de devoir en parler à quiconque, sans parler des policiers.

He continued: "It's deeply personal and quite harrowing to be a victim of rape and sexual assault, and to have to tell anybody about it, let alone police officers.

"Mais je suis content qu'ils aient fait confiance à nous".

"But I'm glad they did. I'm glad they put their trust in us."

La députée Dame Caroline Dinenage a réitéré ses inquiétudes quant à la possibilité pour Brand de publier des vidéos en ligne avant et pendant son procès à ses millions de followers.

MP Dame Caroline Dinenage has re-iterated her concerns about Brand being able to post videos online in the build-up to and during his trial to his millions of followers.

"C'est toujours un problème qu'il a gagné de l'argent tout au long du processus sur sa plateforme de médias sociaux", a-t-elle déclaré à la même émission de télévision de la BBC.

"This does continue to be an issue that he has made money throughout the process on his social media platform," she told the same BBC TV programme.

"C'était très étrange que, au moment où il y avait une enquête policière et des allégations sérieuses dans le journal, il ait pu se connecter à ses deux millions et demi de followers, tout nier et présenter son côté de l'histoire.

"It just felt very odd that during the height of when there was a police investigation and some really serious allegations in the paper, that he was able to go online to his two and a half million followers, denying everything and putting his side of the story forward.

"C'était une opportunité que ses victimes n'avaient pas eu".

"That was an opportunity that his victims didn't have."

Avant d'être conduit dans les cellules, il a dit "Je t'aime" à sa femme Laura Brand, qui était au tribunal.

Brand did not react in the dock as he was sentenced. Before being taken down to the cells, he mouthed "I love you" to his wife Laura Brand, who was in the court.

In September 2023, after the Sunday Times and Channel 4 Dispatches allegations were made against Brand, Culture, Media and Sport (CMS) Committee chair Dame Caroline wrote to social media companies Rumble, X, TikTok and Meta.

Elle a demandé s'ils suivraient YouTube pour couper les revenus de Brand sur leurs plateformes. Rumble a refusé publiquement, et X a déclaré qu'il ne prendrait pas d'action contre les comptes qui n'ont pas enfreint ses règles ou la loi. TikTok a déclaré qu'il n'avait jamais été monétisé là-bas.

She asked if they would follow YouTube in cutting off Brand's income on their platforms. Rumble publicly refused, and X said it does not take action against accounts that have not broken its rules or the law. TikTok said he had never been monetised there.

"Nous savons qu'il avait utilisé ses followers [en ligne] pour tenter de faire taire les gens", a ajouté Mme Caroline.

"We know that he had used his following [online] to try and cow people into silence," added Dame Caroline.

"Et le fait qu'elles [les femmes] aient pu se présenter et raconter leur histoire et dire leur vérité et obtenir cette conviction en raison de cela est incroyablement impressionnant".

"And the fact that they [the women] were able to come forward and tell their story and tell their truth and secure this conviction because of it is unbelievably impressive."
