Le musicien irlandais oscarisé Glen Hansard est décédé dans un accident de moto à Dublin.

Oscar-winning Irish musician Glen Hansard has died in a motorbike crash in Dublin.

L'homme de 56 ans, qui s'est fait connaître lorsqu'il a été choisi pour le film de comédie musicale The Commitments en 1991, a longtemps été le leader du célèbre groupe de rock The Frames.

The 56-year-old, who shot to prominence when he was cast in 1991 musical comedy film The Commitments, was long-time frontman of acclaimed rock band The Frames.

Des hommages ont été rendus à travers le monde de la musique par Ed Sheeran et Bruce Springsteen, qui ont déclaré avoir « le cœur brisé ».

Tributes have been paid across world of music from the likes of Ed Sheeran and Bruce Springsteen, who said he was "heartbroken".

Hansard a eu une riche carrière musicale pendant plus de trois décennies, notamment en remportant l'Oscar de la meilleure chanson originale en 2008 avec Marketa Irglova, qui figurait dans le film romantique musical à petit budget Once.

Hansard had a storied career in music across more than three decades, including winning the Academy Award for Best Original Song in 2008 with Marketa Irglova, which featured in the low-budget musical romance film Once.

Lui et Irglova ont sorti trois albums sous le nom de The Swell Season, tandis que Hansard a également connu le succès en tant qu'artiste solo avec l'album Didn't He Ramble de 2016, nominé pour un Grammy en 2016.

He and Irglova released three albums as The Swell Season, while Hansard also encountered success as a solo artist with 2016 album Didn't He Ramble being nominated for a Grammy in 2016.

L'accident impliquant un seul véhicule s'est produit sur Lower Road à Lucan, à l'ouest de Dublin, et a été signalé aux services d'urgence peu avant 04h30, heure locale.

The single-vehicle crash happened on Lower Road in Lucan, west of Dublin, and was reported to emergency services shortly before 04:30 local time.

"The family is grateful for the support they have received and wishes to thank the emergency services who attended the scene."

Hansard, père d'un garçon de trois ans, a également été salué par des organisations caritatives pour son travail de collecte de fonds pour les sans-abri.

Hansard, the father of a three-year-old boy, has also been hailed by charities for his work in raising money for the homeless.

En 2010, il a lancé un bus annuel pour le réveillon de Noël dans le centre-ville de Dublin, qui a attiré des musiciens de haut niveau, dont Bono, Sinéad O'Connor et Shane McGowan.

In 2010, he started an annual Christmas Eve busk in Dublin City Centre, which attracted high-profile musicians including Bono, Sinéad O'Connor and Shane McGowan.

The Simon Community, a charity in the UK and Ireland which helps homeless people, said it was "devastated" by his death.

"Les talents de Glen seront en effet son héritage, mais son engagement en faveur de la justice sociale le sera également. Nous lui serons éternellement redevables pour la différence qu'il a apportée dans la vie des personnes que nous servons."

"Glen's talents will indeed be his legacy but so too will his commitment to social justice. We will be forever indebted to him for the difference he made in the lives of the people we serve."

Hansard's friend, artist Colin Davidson, said the annual Christmas Eve busk had become "an international Irish institution in its own right".

He said it was "Glen's way of getting him and his friends to, not just shine a light on the problem of homelessness, but to actually do something about it financially".

"He was unique... he was such an incredibly kind, loyal, warm human being who I am proud to call a friend," Davidson said.

Pour ceux qui sont venus à Glen Hansard pour la première fois via sa musique – peut-être après son Oscar ou ses collaborations avec des sommités telles qu'Eddie Vedder – ce sera peut-être une surprise de remonter jusqu'en 1991, à la comédie à succès d'Alan Parker, The Commitments.

For those who first came to Glen Hansard via his music – perhaps after his Oscar win or his collaborations with luminaries such as Eddie Vedder - it may be a surprise to go all the way back to 1991, to Alan Parker's smash-hit Dublin-set comedy The Commitments.

Le voilà, à peine sorti de la vingtaine et avec une touffe de cheveux roux et bouclés, dans le rôle du guitariste décontracté du groupe de soul de la classe ouvrière, Outspan Foster.

There he is, barely out of his twenties and with a shock of curly, red hair, as the working-class soul band's laidback guitarist Outspan Foster.

Le réalisateur du film, Alan Parker, recherchait de vrais musiciens pour composer le groupe fictif, et le jeune homme alors âgé de 20 ans accompagnait un ami aux auditions sans avoir l'intention d'y participer lui-même – mais les producteurs recherchaient un guitariste aux cheveux roux et il y avait le hansard.

The film's director Alan Parker searched for real musicians to make up the fictional band, and the then 20-year-old accompanied a mate to the auditions with no intention of going in himself – but producers were looking for a red-haired guitarist and there was Hansard.

Il n’était pas étonnant qu’il ait été considéré comme parfait pour le rôle : comme Foster, il était un Dublinois du nord, qui avait déjà passé ses premières années à faire la rue dans les rues de sa ville natale.

It was little wonder he was considered right for the role – like Foster, he was a Dubliner from the northside, who had already spent his early years busking on the streets of his home city.

Le concert de Commitments semblait être un hasard et ce niveau de fortune revenait occasionnellement dans la carrière de Hansard, qui dans ses premières années était caractérisée par la greffe typique de tournées et d'enregistrements avec un groupe de rock.

The Commitments gig seemed like serendipity and that level of fortune would come again occasionally in Hansard's career, which in its early years were characterised by the typical graft of touring and recording with a rock band.

Il a formé The Frames un an avant The Commitments, avant d'enregistrer six albums studio, dont Dance The Devil, For The Birds et le top des charts irlandais Burn The Maps.

He formed The Frames one year before The Commitments, going on to record six studio albums including Dance The Devil, For The Birds and Irish chart topper Burn The Maps.

Le groupe avait un culte en dehors de l'île, mais il y était énorme, soutenu par des singles radio-friendly comme Revelate, Pavement Tune et Lay Me Down.

The band had a cult following outside the island but were huge on it, buoyed by radio-friendly singles like Revelate, Pavement Tune and Lay Me Down.

En 2005, il sort un album avec Marketa Irglova sous le titre The Swell Season. En 16 ans, ils enregistreront et sortiront trois albums, mais ce sont leurs exploits cinématographiques qui les propulsent dans la stratosphère.

In 2005, he released an album with Marketa Irglova as The Swell Season. Over the course of 16 years, they would record and release three albums, but it was their exploits on film that sent them stratospheric.

Une fois, la comédie musicale à petit budget dans laquelle ils ont joué, dirigée par l'ancien bassiste de Frames John Carney, est devenue un succès dormant en 2007 avec Falling Slowly, sa chanson phare, remportant un Oscar pour le duo.

Once, the low-budget musical they co-starred in, directed by former Frames bassist John Carney, became a sleeper hit in 2007 with Falling Slowly, its breakout song, winning an Academy Award win for the pair.

Le film, réalisé avec un budget restreint de moins de 100 000 £, a rapporté plus de 20 millions de dollars dans le monde. Steven Spielberg l'a décrit comme lui ayant donné "assez d'inspiration pour durer l'année".

The film, made on a shoestring budget of less than £100,000, went on make more than $20m worldwide. Steven Spielberg described it as a having given him "enough inspiration to last the year".

A Broadway adaptation of the film, featuring Hansard and Irglová's songs, later won eight Tony Awards, including Best Musical.

After 19 years, Hansard's career had reached a new level of fame, with he and Irglova even making a cameo appearance on The Simpsons.

Mais il a maintenu son flux constant de sorties musicales au cours des années qui ont suivi, notamment avec cinq albums solo jusqu'en 2023. Tout ce qui était à l'est est à l'ouest de moi.

But he maintained his steady stream of musical releases in the years since, most notably with five solo albums up to 2023's All That Was East Is West Of Me.

Perhaps his biggest life change came three years ago when he became a father.

He told BBC Radio Ulster in 2023 that it had "in the greatest way possible, upturned everything I know".

"It's absolutely incredible, I can't believe I stood on the other side of this chasm for so long."
