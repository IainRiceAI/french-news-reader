'We're not meant to carry life's challenges alone', Catherine says in first clip

Summary: Catherine has spoken about how "cancer does change you" in a documentary that will be screened on BBC One on 29 November.

Le voyage du cancer de la princesse de Galles sera présenté dans un nouveau documentaire de la BBC "Heal" qui sera diffusé le week-end prochain.

The Princess of Wales' cancer journey is set to be depicted in a new BBC documentary "Heal" that will air next weekend.

Elle suit Catherine annonçant en janvier 2025 qu'elle était en rémission après avoir révélé son diagnostic moins d'un an auparavant.

It follows Catherine announcing in January 2025 that she was in remission after having revealed her diagnosis less than a year prior.

Depuis, elle a parlé ouvertement de sa maladie et de son traitement, mais n'a jamais révélé publiquement quel type de cancer elle avait à affronter.

She has since spoken openly about her illness and treatment although has never disclosed publicly what type of cancer she faced.

Le nouveau documentaire comprendra des images de son arrivée au Three Peaks Challenge plus tôt cette année.

The new documentary will include footage of her completing the Three Peaks Challenge earlier this year.

"Cela change votre corps, cela change votre corps, mais il y a une part beaucoup plus profonde dans ce que le diagnostic de cancer change en vous", dit- elle.

"It changes your body, it changes you physically - but there is a much, much deeper part to what a cancer diagnosis shifts in you," she says.
