# German boat dealer suspected of supplying Channel smuggling gangs, BBC finds

**BBC Original Link:** [https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cqlymky8kpzjo?at_medium=RSS&at_campaign=rss](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cqlymky8kpzjo?at_medium=RSS&at_campaign=rss)
