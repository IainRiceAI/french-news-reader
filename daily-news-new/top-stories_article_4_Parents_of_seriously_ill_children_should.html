Parents of seriously ill children should get paid leave, says commissioner

Children's Commissioner Dame Rachel joins calls for more support for parents and carers

Les parents d'enfants souffrant de maladies ou de handicaps graves devraient avoir droit à un congé payé, a déclaré la commissaire aux enfants, Dame Rachel de Souza.

Parents of children with serious illnesses or disabilities should be entitled to paid leave from work, the children's commissioner Dame Rachel de Souza has said.

[Error Translate] She called on the government to introduce a legal right to 12 weeks of paid leave at 90% of normal pay in order to lift the "immense pressure" and lost earnings families face when a child develops a debilitating condition.

She called on the government to introduce a legal right to 12 weeks of paid leave at 90% of normal pay in order to lift the "immense pressure" and lost earnings families face when a child develops a debilitating condition.

Son intervention intervient alors que le gouvernement continue de mener des consultations sur la manière de soutenir financièrement les familles et les soignants non rémunérés qui s'occupent d'enfants malades en Angleterre, en Écosse et au Pays de Galles.

Her intervention came as the government continued to consult on how to financially support families and unpaid carers looking after ill children in England, Scotland and Wales.

Elle a déclaré : "Les parents ne devraient pas être obligés de choisir entre être au chevet de leur enfant gravement malade et conserver leur emploi."

She said: "Parents should not be forced to choose between being at their seriously ill child's bedside and keeping their job."

Dame Rachel a déclaré que les parents lui ont parlé des "choix impossibles" qu'ils ont faits en "partageant leur temps entre s'occuper de leurs enfants gravement malades ou handicapés et essayer de conserver un emploi".

Dame Rachel said that parents have told her of the "impossible choices" they have made when "splitting time between caring for their seriously ill and disabled children and trying to hold down a job".

[Error Translate] She has also backed a "right to return to work" following extended periods of leave, saying it could help "families whose children have complex or fluctuating needs to remain in employment while providing the care their children need".

Dame Rachel a appelé à ce que le congé non payé pour soignant soit augmenté de cinq à dix jours par an, ainsi qu'à l'introduction d'une forme de congé payé pour soignant.

Dame Rachel called unpaid carer's leave to be increased from five to 10 days a year, and for the introduction of a form of paid carer's leave.

Cela fait suite à la campagne menée par les parents de Hugh Menai-Davis, décédé d’un cancer à l’âge de six ans en 2021.

It comes after campaigning by the parents of Hugh Menai-Davis, who died of cancer aged six in 2021.

[Error Translate] His parents Frances and Ceri Menai-Davis have advocated for a change to employment law that would require employers to guarantee three months' salaries to parents whose child faces serious physical or mental illness.
