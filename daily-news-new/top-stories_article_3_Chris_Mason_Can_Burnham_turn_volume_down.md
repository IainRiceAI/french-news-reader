**🇬🇧 EN:** Meanwhile, what does the European Union itself make of all of this? EU sources see Andy Burnham's desire to "brainstorm" with them what the future relationship might look like as "normal diplomacy" as one figure put it, adding: "I don't think there are many options. And they are clear. The status quo. The customs union. The single market. And full membership."

**🇫🇷 FR:** *En attendant, que fait l'Union européenne elle-même de tout cela ? Et elles sont claires. Le statu quo. L'union douanière. Le marché unique. Et l'adhésion à plein droit. "*

### Section 22

### Section 23

**🇬🇧 EN:** Then, there are the ongoing negotiations between the two sides about unresolved areas of potential cooperation. The EU has made much of its desire to secure a deal on university tuition fees in the next six months, with the hope that EU students will pay no more at UK universities than British students do. The deal would be reciprocal for UK students studying elsewhere in Europe. Both sides think an agreement is within reach. Senior figures in Whitehall are also encouraged by the EU's willingness to include the UK in its 'Made in Europe' plan to protect European industry from China. Then there is the EU's common electricity market.

**🇫🇷 FR:** *Les deux parties ont également exprimé leur souhait de conclure un accord sur les frais de scolarité universitaire dans les six prochains mois, dans l'espoir que les étudiants de l'UE ne paient pas plus dans les universités britanniques que les étudiants britanniques. L'accord serait réciproque pour les étudiants britanniques étudiant ailleurs en Europe.*
