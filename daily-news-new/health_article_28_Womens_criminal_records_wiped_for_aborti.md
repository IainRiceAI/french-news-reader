# Women's criminal records wiped for abortion offences

**BBC Original Link:** [https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cqjd7n37r2r9o?at_medium=RSS&at_campaign=rss](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cqjd7n37r2r9o?at_medium=RSS&at_campaign=rss)  
**Published:** Mon, 16 Nov 2026 11:45:33 GMT  

---

> **BBC Summary:** Women arrested or convicted for having illegal abortions will have their criminal records wiped as new atomatic pardons come into force in England and Wales.

---

### Section 1

**🇬🇧 EN:** Nicola Packer was acquitted of "unlawfully administering to herself a poison or other noxious thing" with the "intent to procure a miscarriage"

**🇫🇷 FR:** *Nicola Packer a été acquittée de " l'administration illégale de soi-même un poison ou d'autres choses nuisibles " avec l'intention d'obtenir un avortement involontaire "*

### Section 2

### Section 3

**🇬🇧 EN:** Ending the Victorian-era law should avoid traumatic experiences such as that of Nicola Packer, who was recently cleared by a jury after taking medication at home during a Covid lockdown. She had ended a pregnancy without realising she was around 26 weeks pregnant, far beyond the legal 10 week limit.

**🇫🇷 FR:** *La fin de la loi de l'ère victorienne devrait éviter des expériences traumatisantes comme celle de Nicola Packer, qui a récemment été acquittée par un jury après avoir pris des médicaments à la maison pendant un lockdown de Covid.*
