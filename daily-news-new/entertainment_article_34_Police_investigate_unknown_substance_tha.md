# Police investigate unknown substance that caused Manchester's Co-op Live evacuation

**BBC Original Link:** [https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c9e851dn8516o?at_medium=RSS&at_campaign=rss](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c9e851dn8516o?at_medium=RSS&at_campaign=rss)

### Section 1

**🇬🇧 EN:** Police are investigating after thousands of people were evacuated from a concert in Manchester following an "unknown substance" being released into the crowd.
