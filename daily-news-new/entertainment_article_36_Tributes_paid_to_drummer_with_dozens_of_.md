# Tributes paid to drummer with dozens of number ones

**BBC Original Link:** [https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c317kjwj18wzo?at_medium=RSS&at_campaign=rss](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c317kjwj18wzo?at_medium=RSS&at_campaign=rss)

### Section 2

**🇬🇧 EN:** Tributes have been paid after the death of drummer Clem Cattini, who performed on several UK Number One singles.

**🇫🇷 FR:** *Des tributs ont été versés après la mort du batteur Clem Cattini, qui a joué sur plusieurs singles numéro un du Royaume-Uni.*

---

### Section 3

**🇬🇧 EN:** The 89-year-old, who lived in Hertfordshire, co-founded the Tornados in 1961. Their instrumental Telstar spent five weeks at the top of the UK chart in 1962, and they became the first British group to top the US Billboard Hot 100.

**🇫🇷 FR:** *Le groupe a été fondateur de Tornados en 1961 et a passé cinq semaines en tête du classement britannique en 1962.*

---

### Section 4

**🇬🇧 EN:** As a session musician, he played on number one hits such as Tom Jones' Green, Green Grass of Home, Dusty Springfield's You Don't Have To Say You Love Me and Tony Christie's (Is This the Way to) Amarillo?.

**🇫🇷 FR:** *En tant que musicien de session, il a joué sur des hits numériques tels que Tom Jones' Green, Green Grass of Home, Dusty Springfield's You Don't Have to Say You Love Me et Tony Christie's (Is This the Way to) Amarillo?.*

---

### Section 5

**🇬🇧 EN:** In a Facebook post, external Christie described Cattini as "a world class musician but also a world class gentleman".

**🇫🇷 FR:** *Dans un message publié sur Facebook, Christie a décrit Cattini comme "un musicien de classe mondiale mais aussi un gentleman de classe mondiale".*

---

### Section 6

**🇬🇧 EN:** He wrote: "So very sad to hear that the great Clem Cattini, drummer of the Tornados and prolific session drummer has passed away. Clem played on more number 1 records than any other British drummer.

**🇫🇷 FR:** *Il a écrit: " C'est très triste d'apprendre que le grand batteur des Tornados, Clem Cattini, qui a joué sur plus de disques que tout autre batteur britannique, est décédé.*

### Section 7

**🇬🇧 EN:** "Proud to have known him. My condolences and love to his family."

---

### Section 8

**🇬🇧 EN:** Carl Palmer, drummer with groups Emerson, Lake & Palmer and Asia, said: "The drummers all over the UK owe you a lot for opening the door for all of us."

**🇫🇷 FR:** *Carl Palmer, batteur des groupes Emerson, Lake & Palmer et Asia, a déclaré: "Les batteurs de tout le Royaume-Uni vous doivent beaucoup pour avoir ouvert la porte à nous tous".*
