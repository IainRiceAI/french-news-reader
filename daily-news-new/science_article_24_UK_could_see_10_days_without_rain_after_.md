**BBC Original Link:** [https://www.bbc.co.uk/weather/articles/cq98zrwd42jzo?at_medium=RSS&at_campaign=rss](https://www.bbc.co.uk/weather/articles/cq98zrwd42jzo?at_medium=RSS&at_campaign=rss)

> **BBC Summary:** With high pressure developing across the UK, the start of November is looking very settled with no significant rain in the forecast.
