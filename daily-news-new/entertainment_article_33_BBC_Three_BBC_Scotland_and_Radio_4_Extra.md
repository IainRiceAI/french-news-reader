# BBC Three, BBC Scotland and Radio 4 Extra broadcast channels to close

**BBC Original Link:** [https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cqx2v800nnr2o?at_medium=RSS&at_campaign=rss](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cqx2v800nnr2o?at_medium=RSS&at_campaign=rss)  
**Published:** Tue, 20 Oct 2026 13:10:43 GMT  

---

> **BBC Summary:** The closures are announced as the corporation continues to try to save £500m over two years.

---

### Section 1

**🇬🇧 EN:** The BBC is to close its BBC Three and BBC Scotland television channels in the spring, as the corporation continues to make £500m of savings over two years.

**🇫🇷 FR:** *La BBC doit fermer ses chaînes de télévision BBC Three et BBC Scotland au printemps, alors que la société continue de faire des économies de 500 millions de livres sterling sur deux ans.*

### Section 2

**🇬🇧 EN:** Both services will become online-only, meaning they will continue to produce and host some content on iPlayer, without the need to fill a terrestrial TV schedule.
