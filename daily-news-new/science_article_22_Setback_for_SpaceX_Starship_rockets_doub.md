# Setback for SpaceX Starship rocket's double-catch test

**BBC Original Link:** [https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/ckregdgnl0xpo?at_medium=RSS&at_campaign=rss](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/ckregdgnl0xpo?at_medium=RSS&at_campaign=rss)

> **BBC Summary:** Elon Musk’s company aborted an attempted catch of the rocket’s booster - instead it came down in the ocean.
