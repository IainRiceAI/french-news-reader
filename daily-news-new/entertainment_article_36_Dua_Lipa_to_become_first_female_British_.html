Coachella a annoncé la formation pour son festival de 2027, Dua Lipa étant la première artiste britannique solo à faire les gros titres.

Coachella has announced the line-up for its 2027 festival, with Dua Lipa set to be the first solo female British artist to headline.

Le chanteur de Training Season est en tête du bilan du vendredi pour les deux week-ends d'avril, le groupe de psych-rock Tame Impala en tête des spectacles du samedi et le chanteur américain SZA qui ferme les deux dimanches.

The Training Season singer is top of Friday's bill for both weekends in April, with psych-rock band Tame Impala headlining the Saturday shows and US singer SZA closing out both Sundays.

Le festival massif revient dans le désert de Californie les 9 à 11 et les 16 à 18 avril de l'année prochaine, avec plus de 100 000 fans attendus chaque jour.

The massive festival returns to the California desert on 9 to 11 and 16 to 18 April next year, with more than 100,000 fans expected to attend each day.

Madonna reviendra au festival après une apparition surprise aux côtés de la vedette de cette année, Sabrina Carpenter, 20 ans après son premier set de Coachella.

Madonna is also set to return to the festival after a surprise appearance alongside this year's headliner Sabrina Carpenter, 20 years on from her first Coachella set.

The Queen of Pop came back with Confessions II, her first album since 2019, earlier this year and had been rumoured to be headlining Glastonbury 2027 before organisers ruled that out.

La star de la pop suédoise Zara Larsson et la chanteuse américaine Phoebe Bridgers sont également sur le point de se produire, ainsi que Diljit Dosanjh qui revient après avoir fait l'histoire en 2023 en devenant la première chanteuse de langue punjabi à se produire au festival.

Swedish pop star Zara Larsson and US singer-songwriter Phoebe Bridgers are also set to perform, as well as Diljit Dosanjh who returns after making history in 2023 by becoming the first Punjabi language singer to perform at the festival.

Jisoo de Blackpink, Kehlani, Weezer et Robyn sont également parmi les artistes de la formation.

Blackpink's Jisoo, US star Kehlani, rock band Weezer and Dancing On My Own singer Robyn are also among the artists on the line-up.

During her Pyramid stage set, Dua, who earlier this year married could-be James Bond actor Callum Turner, also brought out Tame Impala frontman Kevin Parker.

Other UK artists on the 2027 line-up include EsDeeKid, Chase & Status and Jorja Smith.

Coachella is one of the most high-profile music festivals in the world thanks to its line-up and reputation for attracting a celebrity audience.
