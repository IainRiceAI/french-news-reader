Soft launches and late sittings - six ways to get cheaper meals out

Summary: The cost of dining out is getting increasingly hard to swallow - but clever planning can net you cheaper deals.

[Error Translate] Restaurateurs say the rising cost of food, energy, wage and tax bills mean they've had to put up prices, with some top chefs campaigning for government help.

De nombreux restaurants proposent des incitations pour faire franchir les portes aux convives. Voici quelques façons d’économiser de l’argent sur les repas à l’extérieur.

Many eateries are offering incentives to get diners through the doors. Here are some ways to save money on meals out.

[Error Translate] Many restaurants offer points, freebies, discounts and birthday treats to keep customers coming back.

Many restaurants offer points, freebies, discounts and birthday treats to keep customers coming back.

L'un des plus connus est le Pizza Express Club. Les convives réguliers peuvent progresser dans un système bronze, argent et or, débloquant des avantages allant des boules de pâte gratuites aux boissons chaudes et non alcoolisées offertes à la maison.

One of the most well-known is the Pizza Express Club. Regular diners can progress through a bronze, silver and gold system unlocking perks from free dough balls to hot and soft drinks on the house.

[Error Translate] Nando's uses a chilli system for its loyalty programme, while other schemes such as Tastecard offer discounts at hundreds of eat-in and takeaway venues in exchange for an annual fee.

Nando's uses a chilli system for its loyalty programme, while other schemes such as Tastecard offer discounts at hundreds of eat-in and takeaway venues in exchange for an annual fee.

[Error Translate] Restaurants often want to fill seats outside of peak lunch and dinner hours so opting for earlier or later sittings can be rewarded by a cheaper bill.

Restaurants often want to fill seats outside of peak lunch and dinner hours so opting for earlier or later sittings can be rewarded by a cheaper bill.

Des applications telles que First Table, qui fonctionne dans 21 villes du Royaume-Uni, ainsi qu'en Irlande, en Nouvelle-Zélande et en Australie, offrent aux clients une réduction de 50 % sur la nourriture lorsqu'ils réservent le premier ou le dernier créneau de restauration d'un restaurant participant. En échange, les convives paient des frais de réservation minimes.

Apps such as First Table, which operates across 21 UK cities, as well as Ireland, New Zealand and Australia, give diners 50% off food when they book a participating restaurant's first or last dining slot. In exchange, diners pay a small booking fee.

[Error Translate] EatClub is another, giving walk-ins up to 50% off the whole bill at thousands of venues when they redeem an offer within its app. The biggest discounts are at off-peak times.

EatClub is another, giving walk-ins up to 50% off the whole bill at thousands of venues when they redeem an offer within its app. The biggest discounts are at off-peak times.

[Error Translate] Shokofeh Hejazi, director of foresight and innovation at global trends agency The Food People, says earlier dining appeals because it tends to be quieter and calmer, people drink less and it allows them to get home sooner.

Shokofeh Hejazi, director of foresight and innovation at global trends agency The Food People, says earlier dining appeals because it tends to be quieter and calmer, people drink less and it allows them to get home sooner.

[Error Translate] Meeting up for breakfast also tends to be a bit more wallet-friendly as it's usually just one course and diners get the same catch-up time with friends and family, she adds.

Meeting up for breakfast also tends to be a bit more wallet-friendly as it's usually just one course and diners get the same catch-up time with friends and family, she adds.

Pendant les vacances scolaires, de nombreux établissements attireront les familles en proposant des repas gratuits ou à 1 £ aux enfants mangeant avec des adultes payants.

In the school holidays, many places will entice families through their doors by offering free or £1 meals for kids eating with paying adults.

Il s'agit de grandes chaînes telles que TGI Fridays, de cafés dans des supermarchés comme Morrisons et Asda, et même de jardineries comme Dobbies.

These range from big chains such as TGI Fridays, cafes at supermarkets including Morrisons and Asda, and even garden centres like Dobbies.

[Error Translate] While some tie-in with school holidays, other deals are all year round.

While some tie-in with school holidays, other deals are all year round.

De nombreux nouveaux restaurants offriront des réductions dans le but d'encourager les convives à les essayer et à faire connaître leur ouverture d'entreprise.

Many new restaurants will offer discounts in a bid to encourage diners to try them out and spread the word that they've opened for business.

[Error Translate] These are known as "soft launches" and can run for several weeks. They're a cost-effective way of trying a higher-end restaurant without such a hit to the wallet.

These are known as "soft launches" and can run for several weeks. They're a cost-effective way of trying a higher-end restaurant without such a hit to the wallet.

[Error Translate] Hejazi says some restaurants are extending the period before they fully open to garner more buzz, while others are making their soft launch pricing permanent for quieter days such as Mondays.

Hejazi says some restaurants are extending the period before they fully open to garner more buzz, while others are making their soft launch pricing permanent for quieter days such as Mondays.

Les gens ne devraient pas supposer que les prix des établissements gastronomiques leur sont chers, car « il y a des points d'accès pour tout le monde dans beaucoup de ces endroits », dit-elle.

People shouldn't assume they're priced out of fine dining-type venues because "there's access points for everybody in a lot of these places", she says.

Les points de fidélité des supermarchés provenant de ces magasins hebdomadaires s'accumulent au fil du temps, alors pourquoi ne pas les utiliser pour un repas au restaurant ?

Supermarket loyalty points from those weekly shops add up over time so why not put them toward a meal out?

Par exemple, chaque 50 pence gagné en points Tesco Clubcard peut être échangé contre 1,50 £ en bons à dépenser dans les établissements Prezzo italiens participants. La chaîne n'est que l'un des restaurants partenaires de Tesco.

For example, each 50p earned in Tesco Clubcard points can be exchanged £1.50 in vouchers to spend at participating Prezzo Italian venues. The chain is just one of Tesco's restaurant partners.

Enfin, il existe de nombreuses options pour emporter de la nourriture gratuitement ou à une fraction du prix habituel.

Finally, there are plenty of options to take away food for free or at a fraction of the usual price.

Les applications de surplus alimentaires telles que Too Good To Go ou Olio permettent aux clients d'emballer des friandises qui autrement seraient gaspillées.

Surplus food apps such as Too Good To Go, or Olio, allow customers to bag goodies that would otherwise go to waste.

C'est un peu un pari : on ne sait pas toujours précisément ce que l'on va obtenir.

It's a bit of a gamble - you don't always know precisely what you'll be getting.

Mais il y a de toute façon de l'argent à économiser sur les articles nécessaires à l'achat, ce qui représente plus d'argent restant pour le moment où vous décidez de dîner au restaurant.

But there's money to be saved on items needed to buy anyway, that's more cash left over for when you do decide to dine out.
