Parlant lors du lancement de presse, Winkleman a dit qu'elle était totalement concentrée sur les célébrités pendant qu'elle était au château, et qu'elle était collée aux moniteurs montrant des images de ces célébrités tout au long de la journée.

Speaking at the press launch, Winkleman said she is totally focused on the celebrities while she's in the castle, and is glued to the monitors showing footage of them throughout the day.

"Toutes les séries étaient superbes, et quand on fait une série, j'ai cette pièce avec les moniteurs. Je suis comme une étrange qui applique un faux bronzage, en regardant", a-t-elle dit.

"All of them were tremendous, and when we do any series I have this room with the monitors. I'm like a weirdo just applying fake tan, watching," she said.

La présentatrice a ajouté que même quand on lui dit de rentrer chez elle, elle dit aux producteurs "Je ne pars pas", en plaisantant en disant qu'elle a à peine dormi.

The presenter added that even when she's told to go home, she tells the producers "I am not leaving", joking that she got barely any sleep.

Ed Gamble, animateur de la série spin-off de BBC Two Uncloaked, a ajouté qu'il regardait également "des heures de film par jour".

Ed Gamble, host of BBC Two spin-off show Uncloaked, added that he watched "hours of footage a day" as well.

"Quand ils prennent leur petit-déjeuner, je les regarde manger leur petit-déjeuner. C'est vraiment triste... je suis normalement assis dans mon pyjama à manger des céréales, les regardant manger des céréales", a-t-il déclaré à BBC News.

"So when they're eating breakfast, I'm eating breakfast watching them eat breakfast. It's actually quite sad... I'm normally sat there in my pyjamas eating cereal, watching them eat cereal," he told BBC News.

"I like the fact that it is 'human crow meets golf caddy meets the Artful Dodger'. We're pretty happy with it," she joked about this year's outfits.

"I mean, he's just as easily going to be on my side as he is going to twist the knife in my back given the opportunity," he said.

Mathematician Hannah Fry, who called herself "toxically competitive", added she has already looked for "statistical patterns" in the data for previous games; while Little Mix singer Leigh-Anne Pinnock said she took advice from singer Cat Burns, who reached the final as a traitor last year.

"Elle avait un bon conseil. Elle a dit que ça pourrait être intense autour de cette table ronde mais rappelez-vous, c'est juste un jeu", a dit Pinnock.

"She had good advice. She said it might get intense around that round table – but remember, it's just a game," Pinnock said.

"It's breathtaking," Winkleman told the press, adding that she and her family were returning to the Highlands for a holiday.
