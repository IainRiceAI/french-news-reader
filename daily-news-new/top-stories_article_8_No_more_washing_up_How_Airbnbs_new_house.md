# No more washing up: How Airbnb's new house rules have divided hosts and guests

**BBC Original Link:** [https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/ckjwez8g5dyeo?at_medium=RSS&at_campaign=rss](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/ckjwez8g5dyeo?at_medium=RSS&at_campaign=rss)
