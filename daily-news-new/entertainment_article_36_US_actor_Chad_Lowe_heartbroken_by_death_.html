US actor Chad Lowe 'heartbroken' by death of daughter Fiona aged 13

📅 Wed, 30 Sep 2026 08:59:38 GMT 🔗 BBC Original

Summary: Lowe and his wife Kim Painter announce the death of their daughter, who "was the light of our lives".

L'acteur américain Chad Lowe et sa femme, la productrice Kim Painter, ont déclaré qu'ils étaient " profondément attristés " par la mort de leur " bien-aimée " fille de 13 ans, Fiona.

US actor Chad Lowe and his wife, producer Kim Painter, have said they are "deeply heartbroken" following the death of their "beloved" 13-year-old daughter Fiona.

"C'était une jeune fille aimante, intelligente, créative et qui aimait danser", a déclaré la famille dans un communiqué à Page Six, externe et People, externe.

"She was a loving young girl who was smart, creative and loved to dance," the family said in a statement to Page Six, external and People, external.

" Ses sœurs l'adoraient et ses parents l'aimaient profondément.

"She was adored by her sisters and she was deeply loved by her parents. In fact, she was the light of our lives.

"Sa disparition est dévastatrice pour toute notre famille et nous demandons des prières et de l'intimité en ce moment".

"Her passing is devastating for our entire family and we ask for prayers and privacy at this time."

La déclaration conclut: "Si vous ou quelqu'un que vous connaissez souffrez d'une maladie mentale, veuillez contacter la ligne de sauvetage pour les suicides et les crises 988 en appelant ou en envoyant un SMS à 988".

The statement concluded: "If you or someone you know is struggling with mental health, please reach out to the 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline by calling or texting 988."

Chad Lowe, frère de son comédien Rob Lowe, a remporté un Emmy Award en 1993 pour la série télévisée Life Goes On et est apparu dans d'autres programmes, dont Pretty Little Liars et Supergirl.

Chad Lowe, the brother of fellow actor Rob Lowe, won an Emmy Award in 1993 for TV show Life Goes On and has appeared in other programmes including Pretty Little Liars and Supergirl.

Lui et Painter sont également parents de leurs filles Mabel, 17 ans, et Nixie, 10 ans.

He and Painter are also parents to daughters Mabel, 17, and Nixie, 10.
