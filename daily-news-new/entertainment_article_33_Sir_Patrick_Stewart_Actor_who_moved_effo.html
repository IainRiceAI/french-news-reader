Sir Patrick Stewart: Actor who moved effortlessly between Star Trek and Shakespeare

Summary: The English actor began his career on stage before finding fame as the captain of the USS Enterprise.

Sir Patrick Stewart, décédé à l'âge de 86 ans, était un grand acteur de cinéma et de théâtre qui a équilibré la stardom d'Hollywood avec une carrière tout aussi stellaire dans le théâtre classique.

Sir Patrick Stewart, who has died at the age of 86, was a stage and screen great who balanced Hollywood stardom with an equally stellar career in classical theatre.

Alors que de nombreux acteurs sont des types, il a passé sans effort de Shakespeare et Dickens au commandement de l'USS Enterprise et de sa célèbre fauteuil roulant dans X-Men.

While many actors are typecast, he moved effortlessly from Shakespeare and Dickens to the command of the USS Enterprise and his famous wheelchair in X-Men.

Au cours d'une carrière qui a duré sept décennies, Sir Patrick a reçu de nombreux prix, une étoile sur le Hollywood Walk of Fame et un titre de chevalier de la reine Elizabeth II.

In a career that spanned seven decades, Sir Patrick received multiple awards, a star on the Hollywood Walk of Fame and a knighthood from Queen Elizabeth II.

L'un des acteurs les plus polyvalents au monde, il maîtrisait à la fois la comédie et la tragédie, la science-fiction et le drame historique - malgré le dégoût traditionnel de l'industrie du divertissement pour les têtes chauves.

One of the world's most versatile actors, he mastered both comedy and tragedy, science-fiction and historical drama - despite the entertainment industry's traditional dislike of bald heads.

Patrick Hewes Stewart est né à Mirfield, dans le West Riding du Yorkshire, le 13 juillet 1940.

Patrick Hewes Stewart was born in Mirfield in the West Riding of Yorkshire on 13 July 1940.

His mother Gladys was a textile worker. She was active in amateur dramatics and encouraged her three sons to take part.

Son père était un ancien sergent-major du régiment de parachutistes dont l'expérience lors de l'évacuation de Dunkerque en temps de guerre avait réduit sa santé mentale.

His father was a former Sergeant Major in the Parachute Regiment whose experience during the wartime evacuation of Dunkirk had shredded his mental health.

"Mon père était un homme très puissant, se souvient Stewart plus tard. " On disait que, lorsqu'il passait sur le terrain de défilé, les oiseaux cessaient de chanter".

"My father was a very potent individual," Stewart later recalled. "It was said that when he strode on to the parade ground, birds stopped singing."

Mais Alfred Stewart était devenu un homme de colère rapide, souffrant de ce qu'on appellerait maintenant un stress post-traumatique.

But Alfred Stewart had become a quick-tempered man, suffering from what would now be called post traumatic stress.

Il était enclin à boire et il exprimait souvent sa frustration à sa famille, bien que Patrick lui- même n'ait jamais été la cible de ses coups de poing.

He was prone to drink and often took out his frustrations on his family - although Patrick himself was never the target of his fists.

Young Patrick's love of drama developed at school when one of his teachers introduced him to Shakespeare and encouraged him to perform.

His first public performance was in a local church production of Cinderella.

He found the predictability of dramatic performances a welcome change from the turmoil of his home life.

Il quitte l'école à 15 ans et finit par trouver un emploi de journaliste et de rédacteur d'obituaries au Dewsbury Reporter.

He left school at 15 and eventually got a job as a journalist and obituary writer on the Dewsbury Reporter.

Il s'est aussi lancé avec enthousiasme dans le théâtre local florissant. " J'avais l'habitude de réciter Shakespeare par mémoire, " dit- il. " J'ai appris tout ce genre de grands discours. "

He also threw himself enthusiastically into the flourishing local theatre. "I used to recite Shakespeare from memory," he said. "I just learned all these big speeches."

But he found it impossible to concentrate on both his job and his acting. Eventually his editor told him to choose between journalism and the "foolhardy" acting.

He won a scholarship to the Bristol Old Vic and later joined the Royal Shakespeare Company - playing a variety of roles across the company's repertoire.

He was a conspiratorial Roman soldier in the hugely popular I, Claudius and a Soviet spy in Tinker, Tailor, Soldier, Spy and Smiley's People.

He had landed a number of supporting film roles: including playing Guinevere's father in Excalibur, a minstrel in Dune, and a riding instructor in Little Lord Fauntleroy.

But in need of a boost to his finances, Stewart signed a six-year contract, firmly believing the show wouldn't last and he'd be back on the boards before long.
