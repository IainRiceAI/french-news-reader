### Section 3

**🇬🇧 EN:** Even after years of covering results days, I was emotional to see a set of outstanding results in a school facing so many challenges. In fact, the results in the ex-mining town of Hetton-le-Hole were as strong as the most affluent parts of southern England.

### Section 4

**🇬🇧 EN:** This summer, Hetton Academy hit another milestone, with 80% of its pupils getting a pass of at least a grade 4 in maths and English. A grade 4 is roughly equivalent to a 'C' under the old system, and is seen as a passport to jobs and further education.

**🇫🇷 FR:** *Cette année, l'Académie Hetton a franchi une autre étape importante: 80% de ses élèves ont obtenu au moins une note de 4e année en mathématiques et en anglais.*

### Section 5

**🇬🇧 EN:** Another striking detail about Hetton is that almost all of its pupils are from a white, working-class background, a group that's been in the spotlight for some time. **🇫🇷 FR:** *Une autre détail frappant à propos de Hetton est que presque tous ses élèves sont d'origine blanche, de la classe ouvrière, un groupe qui a été au centre des projecteurs pendant un certain temps.
