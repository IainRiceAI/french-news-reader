# White House lifts tariffs on some Canadian goods as midterm elections loom

**BBC Original Link:** [https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c60qk573yzz8o?at_medium=RSS&at_campaign=rss](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c60qk573yzz8o?at_medium=RSS&at_campaign=rss)  
**Published:** Sat, 24 Oct 2026 00:36:47 GMT  

---

> **BBC Summary:** From 30 October, tariffs will be eased on some products, including certain machinery, and placed on others, such as carpets.
