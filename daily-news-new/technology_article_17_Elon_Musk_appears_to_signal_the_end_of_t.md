# Elon Musk appears to signal the end of the road for Cybertruck

**BBC Original Link:** [https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cq1j33w7d232o?at_medium=RSS&at_campaign=rss](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cq1j33w7d232o?at_medium=RSS&at_campaign=rss)  
**Published:** Fri, 06 Nov 2026 21:49:50 GMT  

---

> **BBC Summary:** Musk said it was to make room for production of its Cybercabs - autonomous taxis without a driver.

---

### Section 1

**🇬🇧 EN:** Tesla will wind down production of its Cybertruck vehicles at its Texas factory next year, Elon Musk says.

**🇫🇷 FR:** *Tesla va arrêter la production de ses Cybertruck dans son usine du Texas l'année prochaine, a déclaré Elon Musk.*
