# 'If we don't scream, they won't listen to us': Student protesters speak to BBC in Paris

**BBC Original Link:** [https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/videos/ckg5jqg1npd7o?at_medium=RSS&at_campaign=rss](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/videos/ckg5jqg1npd7o?at_medium=RSS&at_campaign=rss)  
**Published:** Tue, 13 Oct 2026 18:23:22 GMT  

---

> **BBC Summary:** Student protesters in France have told the BBC's James Waterhouse that they will continue to take action until change has been made. The protests have now entered their fourth week.

---

### Section 1

**🇬🇧 EN:** Students protests in France have entered their fourth week and with a renewed impetus, according to the BBC's James Waterhouse.

**🇫🇷 FR:** *Les manifestations étudiantes en France sont entrées dans leur quatrième semaine et avec un nouvel élan, selon James Waterhouse de la BBC.*

---

### Section 2

**🇬🇧 EN:** The nationwide demonstrations against poor classroom conditions, including old buildings, teacher shortages and overcrowded classrooms, have caused disruption to education across France as hundreds of lycées (15-18 year-olds colleges) had to be closed.

**🇫🇷 FR:** *Les manifestations à l'échelle nationale contre les mauvaises conditions des salles de classe, y compris les vieux bâtiments, la pénurie d'enseignants et les salles de classe surpeuplées, ont provoqué des perturbations de l'éducation dans toute la France, car des centaines de lycées (15-18 ans) ont dû fermer leurs établissements.*

---

### Section 3

**🇬🇧 EN:** Students on the streets of Paris told the BBC that they had not achieved anything yet and that stopping now would be a "defeat".

**🇫🇷 FR:** *Des étudiants dans les rues de Paris ont dit à la BBC qu'ils n'avaient encore rien accompli et qu'arrêter maintenant serait une " défaite ".*
