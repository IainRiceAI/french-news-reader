# Musk and Anthropic pledge public share giveaways

**BBC Original Link:** [https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cm2kez327gdko?at_medium=RSS&at_campaign=rss](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cm2kez327gdko?at_medium=RSS&at_campaign=rss)

> **BBC Summary:** Musk's announcement comes after Anthropic pledged to create a public benefit fund ahead of its blockbuster stock market listing.

### Section 1

**🇬🇧 EN:** Elon Musk's SpaceX and artificial intelligence giant Anthropic have pledged big financial giveaways as Big Tech firms come under pressure over the potential impact of AI on humanity.

**🇫🇷 FR:** *SpaceX d'Elon Musk et le géant de l'intelligence artificielle Anthropic ont promis de grands cadeaux financiers alors que les grandes entreprises de technologie sont sous pression sur l'impact potentiel de l'IA sur l'humanité.*

### Section 2
