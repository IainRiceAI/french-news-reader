# Tighter rules planned to protect people from cosmetic surgery that goes badly wrong

**BBC Original Link:** [https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c3x2wxnrpk78o?at_medium=RSS&at_campaign=rss](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c3x2wxnrpk78o?at_medium=RSS&at_campaign=rss)  
**Published:** Tue, 20 Oct 2026 23:06:07 GMT

> **BBC Summary:** There will be restrictions on who can carry out procedures and fines for those who flout the law, in England in 2027.
