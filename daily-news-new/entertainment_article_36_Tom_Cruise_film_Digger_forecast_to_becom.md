# Tom Cruise film Digger forecast to become one of Hollywood's biggest flops

**BBC Original Link:** [https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cke8re5v1pgmo?at_medium=RSS&at_campaign=rss](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cke8re5v1pgmo?at_medium=RSS&at_campaign=rss)  
**Published:** Mon, 12 Oct 2026 14:11:27 GMT

> **BBC Summary:** The film dropped by around 60% in its second weekend, and could become one of Hollywood's biggest flops.

---

### Section 1

**🇬🇧 EN:** Tom Cruise's latest film Digger is expected to lose around $245m (£185m), Hollywood analysts have said, after the film's continuing poor performance at the box office.

**🇫🇷 FR:** *Le dernier film de Tom Cruise Digger devrait perdre environ 245 millions de dollars, selon les analystes hollywoodiens, après la mauvaise performance du film au box-office.*

### Section 2
