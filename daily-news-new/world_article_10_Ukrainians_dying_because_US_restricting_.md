# Ukrainians dying because US restricting intelligence sharing, Zelensky adviser tells BBC

**BBC Original Link:** [https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cmj47yx572z5o?at_medium=RSS&at_campaign=rss](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cmj47yx572z5o?at_medium=RSS&at_campaign=rss)  
**Published:** Mon, 19 Oct 2026 20:49:00 GMT  

---

> **BBC Summary:** Serhii Beskrestnov says there has not been enough time to warn people about incoming missile attacks. The White House said there had been no changes to intelligence sharing.

---

### Section 1

**🇬🇧 EN:** Serhii Beskrestnov said "people have already died" due to lack of warnings about incoming missile attacks

**🇫🇷 FR:** *Serhii Beskrestnov a déclaré que "des gens sont déjà morts" en raison du manque d'avertissements concernant les attaques de missiles entrant*

### Section 2

**🇬🇧 EN:** Ukrainians have died because Kyiv is still not receiving reliable US warnings of incoming Russian attacks, according to a key adviser to President Volodymyr Zelensky.

**🇫🇷 FR:** *Les Ukrainiens sont morts parce que Kiev ne reçoit toujours pas d'avertissements américains fiables sur les attaques russes à venir, selon un conseiller clé du président Volodymyr Zelensky.*

---

### Section 3

**🇬🇧 EN:** Serhii "Flash" Beskrestnov told the BBC that lives had been lost because there had not been time to warn citizens about ballistic missile strikes, saying: "It's dangerous... I think it's really very bad... People [have] already died."

**🇫🇷 FR:** *Serhii "Flash" Beskrestnov a déclaré à la BBC que des vies avaient été perdues parce qu'il n'y avait pas eu de temps pour avertir les citoyens des frappes de missiles balistiques, disant: "C'est dangereux... Je pense que c'est vraiment très mauvais... Des gens [ont] déjà péri".*

### Section 4
