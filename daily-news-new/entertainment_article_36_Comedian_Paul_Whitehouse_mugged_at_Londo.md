> **BBC Summary:** His wife wrote on Instagram that the actor had been "mugged at a cash machine behind a Sainsbury's in Islington".

### Section 1

**🇬🇧 EN:** Actor and comedian Paul Whitehouse has fought back against someone who tried to steal his card at a London cash machine, according to his wife.
