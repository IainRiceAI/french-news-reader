# Doctor burnout putting maternity patients at risk, says regulator

**BBC Original Link:** [https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/ckx28le8dv9wo?at_medium=RSS&at_campaign=rss](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/ckx28le8dv9wo?at_medium=RSS&at_campaign=rss)  
**Published:** Fri, 13 Nov 2026 00:55:31 GMT  

---

> **BBC Summary:** Trainee doctors delivering babies are more likely to suffer from overwork and burnout than other specialties, evidence suggests.

### Section 2

### Section 3

**🇬🇧 EN:** The General Medical Council (GMC) says the fully qualified doctors, who are still training in obstetrics and gynaecology, are reporting more feelings of overwork and sleep deprivation than similar trainees in any other field of medicine or surgery, and are also more wary of escalating concerns.

**🇫🇷 FR:** *Le Conseil général médical (CMM) affirme que les médecins entièrement qualifiés, qui sont toujours formés en obstétrique et en gynécologie, signalent plus de sentiments de surtravaillement et de privation de sommeil que les apprentis similaires dans tout autre domaine de la médecine ou de la chirurgie, et sont également plus prudents face à l'escalade des préoccupations.*
