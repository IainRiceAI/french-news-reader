# Two seriously injured after M20 crash involving more than 30 vehicles in Kent

### Section 4

**🇬🇧 EN:** Eleven people were cared for by paramedics and two taken to hospital, Kent Fire and Rescue Service said.

### Section 5

**🇬🇧 EN:** An air ambulance crew, three fire engines and a "technical rescue unit" were sent to the scene, where National Highways said "thick fog" had been confirmed.

### Section 6

### Section 7
