Ooh, sequel friend! A new Inbetweeners movie is officially in the works, Netflix has confirmed.

The original cast of Simon Bird, James Buckley, Blake Harrison and Joe Thomas will reprise their roles for the feature film, with production starting on Monday.

The Inbetweeners 3 suivra l'histoire de leurs personnages, Will McKenzie, Jay Cartwright, Neil Sutherland et Simon Cooper.

The Inbetweeners 3 will follow on the story of their characters, Will McKenzie, Jay Cartwright, Neil Sutherland and Simon Cooper.

They originally starred in three seasons of the popular coming-of-age sitcom, which aired on E4 between 2008 and 2010.

Il faisait suite à leurs essais et mésaventures en tant qu'adolescents de banlieue au Rudge Park Comprehensive fictif alors qu'ils naviguaient dans la vie scolaire et l'amitié.

It followed their trials and misadventures as suburban teenagers at the fictional Rudge Park Comprehensive as they navigated school life and friendship.

The show has gained new fans over the years due to some of its best bits finding new life on social media, as well as through the film sequels.

The third instalment follows two sequels backed by Film4, which were released in 2011 and 2014.

Production kicks off after months of speculation that there would be another film, but beyond the lead characters returning, the rest of the cast is yet to be confirmed.

However, Netflix has announced The Inbetweeners creators, Damon Beesley and Iain Morris, will return to write and direct the film.

Beesley and Morris said it has been "super fun" hanging out with friends again.

"We're going to work hard every week to ensure there are no problemos making this new film for humans," they added.

Le premier film est entré dans l'histoire en tant que plus grand week-end d'ouverture pour une comédie britannique à l'époque, selon Netflix, tandis que la suite a également battu des records au box-office.

The first film made history as the biggest opening weekend for a UK comedy at the time, according to Netflix, while the sequel also broke box office records.
