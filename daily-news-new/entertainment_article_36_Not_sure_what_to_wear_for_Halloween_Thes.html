Summary: Freaky Nikki, Dolly Parton and whimsical costumes like gargoyles are popular among adults dressing up this year.

Si vous cherchez toujours le costume d'Halloween parfait, alors vous voudrez peut-être vous inspirer de gargoyles, de Freaky Nikki et de Dolly Parton - ils seront tous des choix de tenue populaires pour 2026.

If you're still on the hunt for the perfect Halloween costume, then you might want to look to gargoyles, Freaky Nikki and Dolly Parton for inspiration - they are all predicted to be popular outfit choices for 2026.

"People don't necessarily want to look frightening - they want to look extraordinary," says costume designer Tamar Zaig, who runs a fancy dress hire shop in London and says she's seeing a "real appetite for escapism, glamour and a bit of dark humour" right now.
