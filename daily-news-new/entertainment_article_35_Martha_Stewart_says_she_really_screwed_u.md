# Martha Stewart says she 'really screwed up' with homeless Halloween costume

**BBC Original Link:** [https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c8zxle6qq4q0o?at_medium=RSS&at_campaign=rss](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c8zxle6qq4q0o?at_medium=RSS&at_campaign=rss)  
**Published:** Mon, 02 Nov 2026 09:36:45 GMT  

---

> **BBC Summary:** The veteran lifestyle guru has deleted a photo of herself wearing torn clothes and dirt on her face.

---

### Section 1

**🇬🇧 EN:** Veteran lifestyle guru Martha Stewart has admitted she "really screwed up" by dressing as a homeless person for Halloween.

**🇫🇷 FR:** *Martha Stewart, une ancienne gourou du style de vie, a admis avoir " vraiment foiré " en s'habillant comme une personne sans abri pour Halloween.*

---

### Section 2

**🇬🇧 EN:** The 85-year-old US entrepreneur and personality has deleted a photo of herself wearing torn clothes and dirt on her face, holding a sign saying: "I am hungry help me!!"

**🇫🇷 FR:** *L'entrepreneure et personnalité américaine de 85 ans a supprimé une photo d'elle-même portant des vêtements déchirés et de la saleté sur son visage, tenant un panneau disant: "J'ai faim, aidez-moi!!"*

---

### Section 3

**🇬🇧 EN:** She posted a statement on Sunday saying she was "distressed that my Halloween costume yesterday, which was intended to bring more awareness to the very serious subject of homelessness, has offended people, which was never my intention".

**🇫🇷 FR:** *Elle a publié un communiqué dimanche disant qu'elle était "triste que mon costume d'Halloween hier, qui visait à sensibiliser davantage les gens au sujet très grave du sans-abri, ait offensé les gens, ce qui n'a jamais été mon intention".*

---

### Section 4

**🇬🇧 EN:** That was followed by a fuller apology. "It's hard to admit when you've really screwed up. My earlier statement didn't go far enough. I know many people are suffering economically. Wearing a costume like I did was insensitive and not funny. I am very sorry."

**🇫🇷 FR:** *"C'est difficile d'admettre que vous avez vraiment tout gâché. Mon précédent commentaire n'a pas été suffisamment clair. Je sais que beaucoup de gens souffrent économiquement.*

---
