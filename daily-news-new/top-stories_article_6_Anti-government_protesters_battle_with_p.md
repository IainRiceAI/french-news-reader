# Anti-government protesters battle with police in Albania

**BBC Original Link:** [https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/crwydey50xeno?at_medium=RSS&at_campaign=rss](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/crwydey50xeno?at_medium=RSS&at_campaign=rss)  
**Published:** Sat, 17 Oct 2026 20:53:00 GMT  

---

> **BBC Summary:** There were violent clashes between demonstrators and riot police throughout Saturday in the capital Tirana.

---

### Section 1

**🇬🇧 EN:** Demonstrators and riot police fought pitched battles in the centre of Tirana on Saturday for the fifth day in a row.

**🇫🇷 FR:** *Les manifestants et la police antiémeutes ont livré des batailles intenses dans le centre de Tirana samedi pour le cinquième jour consécutif.*

---

### Section 2

**🇬🇧 EN:** Masked protesters, many of them high school and university students, hurled rocks and petrol bombs at riot police trying to defend the education ministry, one of the focal points of the protests.

**🇫🇷 FR:** *Des manifestants masqués, dont beaucoup d'étudiants du secondaire et de l'université, ont lancé des pierres et des bombes à essence contre la police antiémeute pour défendre le ministère de l'Éducation, l'un des centres de protestation.*

### Section 3
