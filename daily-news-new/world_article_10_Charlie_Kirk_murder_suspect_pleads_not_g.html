Charlie Kirk murder suspect pleads not guilty as judge clears way for death penalty trial

📅 Wed, 02 Sep 2026 01:59:10 GMT 🔗 BBC Original

Summary: The 23-year-old is charged with aggravated murder, a capital offence in Utah, and six other counts.

[Error Translate] A 23-year-old Utah man has pleaded not guilty to the murder of conservative activist Charlie Kirk – as a judge ruled prosecutors can seek the death penalty at trial.

A 23-year-old Utah man has pleaded not guilty to the murder of conservative activist Charlie Kirk – as a judge ruled prosecutors can seek the death penalty at trial.

Tyler Robinson nie le meurtre aggravé, un crime passible de la peine capitale dans l'Utah et six autres accusations en lien avec la fusillade de Kirk lors d'un événement sur le campus le 10 septembre 2025.

Tyler Robinson denies aggravated murder, a capital offence in Utah, and six other charges in connection with the shooting of Kirk at a campus event on 10 September 2025.

[Error Translate] Defence lawyers had tried to have the death penalty taken off the table, but prosecutors argued that aggravating factors, such as firing into a crowd in the presence of children, warranted a capital trial.

Defence lawyers had tried to have the death penalty taken off the table, but prosecutors argued that aggravating factors, such as firing into a crowd in the presence of children, warranted a capital trial.

[Error Translate] Turning Point USA co-founder Kirk, 31, was shot in the neck while speaking at Utah Valley University in Orem.

Turning Point USA co-founder Kirk, 31, was shot in the neck while speaking at Utah Valley University in Orem.

Kirk a été reconnu pour avoir contribué à propulser le vote des jeunes pour Trump

Kirk was credited with helping propel the youth vote for Trump

L'accusé, vêtu d'un costume gris et consultant occasionnellement ses avocats, a comparu mardi devant le tribunal de la ville de Provo.

The defendant, wearing a grey suit and occasionally consulting with his lawyers, appeared in court in the city of Provo on Tuesday.

La veuve de Kirk, Erika, la mère de leurs deux enfants, et ses parents, ⁠Robert et Kathryn, étaient également présents à l'audience.

Kirk's widow, Erika, the mother of their two children, and his parents, ⁠Robert and Kathryn, were also at the hearing.

[Error Translate] In a statement shared afterwards, the Kirk family said the judge's decision "marks an important step" in their "pursuit of justice".

In a statement shared afterwards, the Kirk family said the judge's decision "marks an important step" in their "pursuit of justice".

"Chaque étape de ce processus porte le poids de tout ce que le meurtre de Charlie a enlevé à sa famille, en particulier à ses enfants qui grandiront sans leur père", ajoute le communiqué.

"Every step in this process carries the weight of all that Charlie's murder has taken from his family, especially his children who will grow up without their father," the statement added.

Les plaidoiries finales ont suivi une audience préliminaire de cinq jours en juillet, au cours de laquelle les procureurs ont présenté leurs preuves pour démontrer la cause probable du procès.

Closing arguments followed a five-day preliminary hearing in July, during which prosecutors outlined their evidence to show probable cause for trial.

Cela comprenait l'ADN qui aurait lié Robinson à l'arme du crime et à d'autres objets récupérés sur les lieux ; les aveux qu'il aurait faits à ses proches ; et une déclaration vidéo enregistrée de son colocataire et partenaire romantique, Lance Twiggs.

That included DNA allegedly tying Robinson to the murder weapon and other items recovered at the scene; confessions he allegedly made to loved ones; and a taped video statement from his roommate and romantic partner, Lance Twiggs.

"Il existe une montagne de preuves qui prouvent qu'il est le tireur", a déclaré mardi le procureur Ryan McBride au tribunal, en citant une vidéo qui, selon lui, montrait Robinson "habillé pour tuer" sur le campus le jour de la fusillade.

"There is a mountain of evidence that proves that he is the shooter," prosecutor Ryan McBride told the court on Tuesday, outlining video that he said showed Robinson "dressed to kill" on campus on the day of the shooting.

"Nous savons que l'accusé est le tireur... premièrement, grâce aux preuves vidéo ; deuxièmement, grâce à l'ADN ; troisièmement, grâce à ses aveux ; et quatrièmement, grâce aux preuves indirectes", a déclaré McBride.

"We know that the defendant is the shooter… first, because of the video evidence; second, because of the DNA; third, because of his admissions; and fourth, because of circumstantial evidence," McBride said.

[Error Translate] Prosecutors said the accused, a former apprentice electrician, was in a relationship with his roommate and regarded Kirk's conservative views, particularly on transgender issues, as "repugnant" - which they argued was the motive.

Prosecutors said the accused, a former apprentice electrician, was in a relationship with his roommate and regarded Kirk's conservative views, particularly on transgender issues, as "repugnant" - which they argued was the motive.

Mardi, les procureurs ont également évoqué des facteurs "aggravants", accusant l'accusé d'avoir "joué avec la vie d'autrui" lorsqu'il aurait tiré sur Kirk, qui était entouré d'autres personnes.

On Tuesday, prosecutors also outlined "aggravating" factors - accusing the defendant of "gambling with the lives of others" when he allegedly fired towards Kirk, who was surrounded by other people.

"Il jouait en fait à une partie de roulette balistique", a déclaré le procureur Chad Grunander.

"He was playing, in effect, a game of ballistic roulette," prosecutor Chad Grunander said.

"Cela fait 356 jours que Tyler Robinson a assassiné Charlie Kirk", a ajouté Grunander. "Il est temps pour lui, l'accusé, d'être jugé et mis en accusation. Aujourd'hui."

"It's been 356 days since Tyler Robinson assassinated Charlie Kirk," Grunander added. "It's time for him, the defendant, to be bound over for trial and arraigned. Today."

Les avocats de la défense ont tenté de semer le doute sur les preuves à charge.

Defence lawyers attempted to sow doubt about the prosecution's evidence.

Lors de l'audience de juillet, ils ont soulevé des questions sur la procédure et les qualifications des experts qui ont témoigné. Les avocats de Robinson se sont opposés à plusieurs reprises aux caméras retransmettant en direct les débats judiciaires.

During July's hearing, they raised questions about procedure and the credentials of experts who testified. Robinson's lawyers have repeatedly objected to cameras livestreaming court proceedings.

Le juge Tony Graf a de nouveau écouté les arguments sur la question lors d'une audience à huis clos mardi matin avant le début des plaidoiries – mais il s'est prononcé en faveur de la transparence dans une affaire qui a suscité des théories du complot.

Judge Tony Graf again listened to arguments on the issue during a sealed hearing on Tuesday morning before closing arguments began - but he ruled in favour of transparency in a case that has sparked conspiracy theories.

Mardi, la défense a cherché à minimiser les facteurs aggravants évoqués par les procureurs, arguant qu'aucune balle n'avait touché qui que ce soit dans la foule à l'exception de Kirk.

On Tuesday, the defence sought to downplay the aggravating factors outlined by prosecutors - arguing that no bullets hit anyone in the crowd apart from Kirk.

Le tribunal a entendu et vu lors de l'audience de juillet comment l'accusé aurait inspecté le campus à plusieurs reprises le jour où Kirk a été abattu - interagissant avec des membres de la TPUSA et mangeant du Chick-fil-A avant de revenir dans des vêtements différents.

The court heard and saw during July's hearing how the accused allegedly scouted the campus repeatedly on the day Kirk was shot - interacting with TPUSA members and eating Chick-fil-A before returning in different clothes.

La nouvelle tenue comprenait "un pantalon long pour masquer le fusil qu'il avait caché dans son pantalon", a déclaré McBride mardi.

The new outfit included "long pants to obscure the rifle he had concealed down his pants", McBride said on Tuesday.

[Error Translate] According to prosecutors' evidence presented during the July hearing, Robinson's DNA was found on his grandfather's rifle stashed in a wooden area near campus after the shooting, as well as on a towel it was wrapped in and a screwdriver recovered at the scene.

According to prosecutors' evidence presented during the July hearing, Robinson's DNA was found on his grandfather's rifle stashed in a wooden area near campus after the shooting, as well as on a towel it was wrapped in and a screwdriver recovered at the scene.

[Error Translate] McBride also told the court on Tuesday that engravings found on casings from the shooting allegedly matched a Dremel tool recovered at the home the defendant shared with his roommate.

L'entretien vidéo de Twiggs avec les procureurs a été diffusé en juillet lors de l'audience. Le colocataire de Robinson a témoigné que l'accusé avait avoué la fusillade, pleuré et dit qu'il aurait souhaité ne pas l'avoir fait.

Twiggs' videotaped interview with prosecutors was played in July during the hearing. Robinson's roommate testified that the defendant had confessed to the shooting, cried and said he wished he hadn't done it.

Robinson, en compagnie de sa famille et d'un voisin, s'est rendu le lendemain du meurtre.

Robinson, in the company of his family and a neighbour, turned himself in the day after the murder.

Il doit comparaître au tribunal le 23 octobre, date à laquelle la date de son procès devrait être fixée.

He is next due in court on 23 October, when a trial date is expected to be set.
