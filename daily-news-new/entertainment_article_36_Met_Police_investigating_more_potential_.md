# Met Police investigating more 'potential criminal allegations' about Brand

**BBC Original Link:** [https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cvwy7ydg4264o?at_medium=RSS&at_campaign=rss](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cvwy7ydg4264o?at_medium=RSS&at_campaign=rss)  
**Published:** Tue, 10 Nov 2026 14:09:11 GMT  

---

> **BBC Summary:** Det Supt Andy Furphy says the force had received "several reports during the trial and since the trial".

---

### Section 1

**🇬🇧 EN:** The Metropolitan Police have said several people contacted them during the trial and following the conviction and sentencing of the disgraced comedian Russell Brand, "to provide information or report a potential criminal allegation".

**🇫🇷 FR:** *La police métropolitaine a déclaré que plusieurs personnes les ont contactées pendant le procès et après la condamnation et la condamnation du comédien Russell Brand, "pour fournir des informations ou signaler une allégation criminelle potentielle".*

---

### Section 2

**🇬🇧 EN:** A Met spokesperson said: "Detectives are assessing this information as part of their ongoing enquiries."

**🇫🇷 FR:** *Un porte-parole du Met a déclaré: "Les détectives évaluent ces informations dans le cadre de leurs enquêtes en cours".*

---

### Section 3

**🇬🇧 EN:** Speaking to BBC Breakfast, Det Supt Andy Furphy, who led the Brand investigation, said officers were "looking at exactly what we've got" and "whether they're information that points to behaviour or whether they could constitute a criminal offence".

**🇫🇷 FR:** *S'adressant à BBC Breakfast, le détective Andy Furphy, qui a dirigé l'enquête sur Brand, a déclaré que les agents " regardaient exactement ce que nous avions " et " s'il s'agissait d'informations qui indiquaient un comportement ou s'il pouvait constituer une infraction pénale ".*
