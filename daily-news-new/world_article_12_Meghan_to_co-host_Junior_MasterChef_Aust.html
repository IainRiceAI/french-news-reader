La duchesse de Sussex a été confirmée comme co-animatrice de la nouvelle saison de Junior MasterChef Australia.

The Duchess of Sussex has been confirmed as a co-host of the new season of Junior MasterChef Australia.

Meghan rejoindra la série 2027 en tant qu'"invitée spéciale" avec son compagnon hôte Poh Ling Yeow, selon la chaîne australienne Channel 10.

Meghan will join the 2027 series as a "special guest" with fellow host Poh Ling Yeow, according to Australia's Channel 10.

La duchesse a déclaré qu'elle "ne pouvait pas attendre" de participer au programme après une apparition en tant que juge invité sur MasterChef Australia l'année dernière.

The duchess said she "can't wait" to take part in the programme after an appearance as a guest judge on MasterChef Australia last year. It was the show's largest Sunday audience since 2023, according to the statement from Paramount ANZ.

L'annonce intervient alors que Meghan fait face à un examen accru en raison de l'accusation de maltraitance sexuelle d'un enfant de son père Thomas Markle.

The announcement comes as Meghan faces increased scrutiny due to her father Thomas Markle being accused of child sexual abuse. He denies the allegations.

La BBC a appris que Meghan avait rompu le contact avec son père l'année dernière, lui disant qu'elle et les autorités savaient qu'il avait abusé de jeunes filles.

The BBC understands Meghan broke contact with her father last year, telling him she and authorities knew he had abused young girls.

Markle a fermement rejeté les accusations comme étant des " mensonges " qui sont utilisées pour détruire sa réputation.

Markle has strongly rejected the accusations as "lies" which are being used to destroy his reputation.

Meghan's MasterChef debut was announced on the second day of the tour, a Wednesday on which the duchess had been notably absent from public-facing appearances. That evening, it was revealed she had been filming an episode of the programme.

La chaîne australienne Network 10, qui diffuse la série dans le cadre de Paramount ANZ, n'a pas répondu à une question de la BBC sur le fait que Meghan serait payée pour co-animer la saison Junior MasterChef.

It is understood she was not paid for her appearance. Australia's Network 10, which airs the series as part of Paramount ANZ, did not respond to a question from the BBC about whether Meghan would be paid for co-hosting the Junior MasterChef season.

"J'adore cuisiner avec mes enfants, donc voir ce que ces jeunes stars créent dans la cuisine va être très amusant", a déclaré Meghan dans un communiqué.

"I love cooking with my own kids, so seeing what these young stars create in the kitchen is going to be a lot of fun," Meghan said in a statement.

In August, the Sussexes announced they were moving back to the UK from California. The couple left the UK and stepped back from their royal duties in early 2020, before settling in California in March that year.

She also launched lifestyle brand As Ever, which includes a food range, but in March it ended its partnership with the streaming platform.
