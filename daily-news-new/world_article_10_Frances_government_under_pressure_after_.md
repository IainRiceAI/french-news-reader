# France's government under pressure after weekend of widespread violence

**BBC Original Link:** [https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/crwyd02yz22vo?at_medium=RSS&at_campaign=rss](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/crwyd02yz22vo?at_medium=RSS&at_campaign=rss)  
**Published:** Mon, 19 Oct 2026 04:16:46 GMT

> **BBC Summary:** Political tension grips France after rioting marred protests over the cost of living and struggling public services, Katya Adler writes.

**🇬🇧 EN:** President Emmanuel Macron urged calm in his national address following widespread looting and violence

**🇫🇷 FR:** *Le président Emmanuel Macron a exhorté à la sérénité dans son discours national à la suite de pillages et de violences généralisés*

### Section 2

**🇬🇧 EN:** France's Prime Minister Sébastien Lecornu is holding a crisis cabinet meeting on Monday to assess a weekend of violence and looting across the country, in what were some of the worst scenes of public disorder in modern French history.

**🇫🇷 FR:** *Le Premier ministre français Sébastien Lecornu tient lundi une réunion de crise du cabinet pour évaluer un week-end de violence et de pillage dans tout le pays, dans ce qui a été l'une des pires scènes de désordre public de l'histoire française moderne.*

---

### Section 3

**🇬🇧 EN:** On Saturday over half a million mainly peaceful protesters demonstrated against rising living costs and dwindling access to decent public services, including a good state education. But the protests turned nasty after groups of black-clad, masked activists and vandals appeared.

**🇫🇷 FR:** *Samedi, plus d'un demi-million de manifestants, principalement pacifiques, ont manifesté contre la hausse des coûts de la vie et la diminution de l'accès à des services publics décent, y compris une bonne éducation publique.*

---

### Section 4

**🇬🇧 EN:** An estimated 1,500 shops and businesses were looted and destroyed. Riot police fired tear gas and turned water cannon on crowds. They were seen hitting people with batons in several places. France's interior ministry said about 1,000 police officers were injured on Saturday, seven of them seriously.

**🇫🇷 FR:** *Les autorités françaises ont déclaré que les manifestants avaient été blessés samedi, et que sept d'entre eux étaient gravement blessés.*

---

### Section 5

**🇬🇧 EN:** There had been rumours in France that President Emmanuel Macron may introduce a temporary curfew in parts of the country, or even impose a state of emergency to help calm the febrile atmosphere. In the end, he used a televised address to the nation on Sunday to call for national unity and to try to assure the French public that the perpetrators of the violence would be punished.

**🇫🇷 FR:** *Il y a eu des rumeurs en France que le président Emmanuel Macron pourrait imposer un couvre-feu temporaire dans certaines parties du pays, voire imposer un état d'urgence pour aider à calmer l'atmosphère fébrile.*

### Section 16

**🇫🇷 FR:** *Le président Macron est considéré ici comme un président paralysé. Il est en train de sortir.
