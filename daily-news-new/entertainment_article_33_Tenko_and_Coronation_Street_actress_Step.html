L'actrice Stephanie Cole, connue pour ses rôles dans Tenko, Open All Hours et Coronation Street, est morte à l'âge de 84 ans.

Actress Stephanie Cole, known for roles in Tenko, Open All Hours and Coronation Street, has died aged 84.

Une déclaration de son agent John Grant, émise au nom de sa famille, confirme que l'actrice vivait avec la maladie d'Alzheimer depuis plusieurs années.

A statement from her agent John Grant, issued on behalf of her family, confirmed the actress had been living with Alzheimer's for several years.

" Stephanie était un talent extraordinaire dont la longue et distinguée carrière comprenait de nombreuses représentations emblématiques dans le théâtre et la télévision ", lit-on dans le communiqué.

"Stephanie was an extraordinary talent whose long and distinguished career included many iconic performances in theatre and television," the statement read.

" Elle a apporté de la joie à des millions de personnes grâce à ses nombreux rôles bien-aimés dans les sitcoms et les drames.

"She brought joy to millions through her many much-loved roles in sitcom and drama. She will also be remembered for her tireless work supporting mental health charities."

Solihull-born Cole, who trained at Bristol Old Vic Theatre School, after making her stage debut at the age of 17 – playing the 90-year-old medium Madame Arcati in Noel Coward's Blithe Spirit.

She went on to enjoy a lengthy career that spanned TV, radio and film and was perhaps best known for playing Dr Beatrice Mason in the 1980s BBC wartime TV drama Tenko.

The series chronicled the lives of British women in Singapore after the Japanese invasion and their consequent confinement in a Japanese prisoner of war camp.

Cole, in her breakout role, played a doctor who struggled to look after the women's health, opposite Ann Bell and Claire Oberman.
