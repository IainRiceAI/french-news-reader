# Governments 'asleep at the wheel' over social care, says head of review

**BBC Original Link:** [https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c9143zxyyvndo?at_medium=RSS&at_campaign=rss](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c9143zxyyvndo?at_medium=RSS&at_campaign=rss)  
**Published:** Wed, 11 Nov 2026 09:08:48 GMT  

---

> **BBC Summary:** Baroness Casey criticises variability of services and fees charged to self-funders in England.

---

### Section 1

**🇬🇧 EN:** Successive governments have been "asleep at the wheel" on adult social care, says the head of the independent commission looking into the sector in England.

**🇫🇷 FR:** *Les gouvernements successifs ont "dormi au volant" des soins sociaux pour adultes, dit le chef de la commission indépendante qui examine le secteur en Angleterre.*

### Section 2

**🇬🇧 EN:** Baroness Louise Casey said care services for older people and adults with disabilities had been developed in a largely uncontrolled and unintended way, unlike the deliberate design of the NHS in 1948.

**🇫🇷 FR:** *La baronne Louise Casey a déclaré que les services de soins pour les personnes âgées et les adultes handicapés avaient été développés de manière largement incontrôlée et involontaire, contrairement à la conception délibérée du NHS en 1948.*

### Section 5
