# Hyrox athlete who soiled herself in race says she put others at risk

**BBC Original Link:** [https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c6gr8l15k744o?at_medium=RSS&at_campaign=rss](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c6gr8l15k744o?at_medium=RSS&at_campaign=rss)  
**Published:** Thu, 29 Oct 2026 23:03:39 GMT  

---

> **BBC Summary:** In her first interview since the incident, Joanna Wietrzyk told the BBC she takes responsibility for it but did not deserve such a severe backlash.

---

### Section 1

**🇬🇧 EN:** An Australian athlete has admitted putting others at risk by continuing to race after soiling herself during an endurance sports event.

**🇫🇷 FR:** *Une athlète australienne a admis mettre les autres en danger en continuant sa course après s'être souillée lors d'un événement d'endurance.*

---

### Section 2

**🇬🇧 EN:** Speaking exclusively to the BBC ahead of her return to the sport, Joanna Wietrzyk suggested her determination to win the Hyrox race in Beijing in September had clouded her judgement: "It was my decision to continue racing so I take full responsibility for that."

**🇫🇷 FR:** *Parlant exclusivement à la BBC avant son retour au sport, Joanna Wietrzyk a suggéré que sa détermination à gagner la course Hyrox à Pékin en septembre avait éclipsé son jugement: "C'était ma décision de continuer à courir et je prends toute la responsabilité de cela".*

---

### Section 3

**🇬🇧 EN:** Challenged over why she carried on, she said: "I didn't really think about those around me… the decision that I made in hindsight was the wrong decision and I'm truly sorry for everyone that I did affect."

**🇫🇷 FR:** *Interrogée sur la raison pour laquelle elle a continué, elle a dit: "Je n'ai pas vraiment pensé aux gens autour de moi... la décision que j'ai prise en arrière-plan était la mauvaise décision et je suis vraiment désolée pour tous ceux que j'ai affectés".*

---

### Section 4

**🇬🇧 EN:** The 24-year-old said that the experience and subsequent backlash was "traumatic". She also said she had considered walking away from the sport, in which she holds a world record.
